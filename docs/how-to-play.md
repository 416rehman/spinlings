# How to play

The complete player guide. Every number here is the one the game plays by, and a test checks each against the code. [spinlings.dev/odds](https://spinlings.dev/odds) publishes every rate, and [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) has the design behind them.

There are no daily quotas anywhere. Every finished battle pays, and the only waiting you can meet is the spacing between battles and a full pack bank.

There is nothing to set up either. After installing, the game starts on its own the first time you open Claude Code, and `/spin` is optional.

## Where the game lives

- **The band** sits above the prompt and is hidden unless something is live: a battle, a result, a catch choice, a pack that just charged, or a gift.
- **The pane** opens with `/spin`. Its four tabs sit on hotkeys 1 to 4: **Team** (your three slots, resting timers, rating, sparks and notices), **Collection** (your cards and each card's details), **Discoveries** (this season's species and your Fusion Log) and **Community**. Its header shows waiting packs, a pack that fills with a labelled countdown such as `Next pack 18m`, and the daily rule: press that rule to see its effect. Press Help for a short field guide; Back or Close always has its own button.
- **The status line** shows which world you are in (`spinlings · Online` or `spinlings · Offline`), any packs waiting (`spinlings · Online · 2 packs`), and the battle while one is on (`spinlings · vs soft-otter-42`, `spinlings · wild Fogmaw`). It never shows sparks, and `/spin quiet` empties it.

Community opens on **Profile**, with your current stats, passkey and settings controls, and one browser link. **Open your collection** appears when the server supports browser collections; older servers offer **View public profile**. Its section bar switches between **Profile**, **Market**, **Rankings** and **Trading** in place. Market and Rankings appear where the online server supports them; Trading has offers, the trade board, the Wandering Trader and gifts. Offline, Profile and the Trader remain available. Card, listing and player details open over the selected section, so Back returns you there.

`/spin quiet` silences everything. `/spin sound on` turns on tiny chimes for rare reveals, legendaries, evolutions and first discoveries; it is off by default, and the chimes are generated by a script in this repo. `/spin motion off` makes every animation instant.

**Reading your team.** Pebble is your league; the number labelled rating measures your duel standing. Sparks buy packs, crafting and fusion. A win streak counts consecutive wins, with a bonus pack every third win. Green, blue, orange and purple marks belong to the Haiku, Sonnet, Opus and Fable families; cards show their family and rarity names beside the art.

## Battles

### When they happen

- **Wild encounters find you while Claude works.** Once Claude's main turn has run for 20 seconds, each further 15 seconds has a 30% chance of an encounter, at most once every 8 minutes. You need at least one card that is not tired. A wild team has 1 to 3 creatures, common 78%, rare 18% and epic 4%, within 1 level of your team's average. Your very first encounter is guaranteed at 20 seconds and is a single gentle creature, and your first wild win always catches.
- **Some of those are duels.** An encounter is a duel 40% of the time when an opponent is available and you have not dueled in the last 20 minutes.
- **`/spin battle`** starts a duel any time, at most once every 2 minutes. There is no command for wild encounters.
- **One at a time.** Starting a battle while another is unfinished settles the old one first, as if you never pressed.
- **Rested bonus.** After 4 or more hours without a battle, the first wild encounter when you come back is guaranteed rare or better.

Battles never wait for Claude, and Claude never waits for a battle. Every round plays at 2.2 seconds.

### How a battle runs

- Your team has 3 slots, and slot order is play order. The active creature is the first one still standing.
- Each round, both active creatures act, the faster one first. A normal attack builds charge; 2 charges make the special ready, and it fires on its own.
- When your special fires, the band shows `[1] Now!`. Press 1 during that round for a **Perfect** special at 1.3x power. Watching earns a little; looking away costs nothing.
- Damage depends on attack and defense, the family matchup, the arena, a little variance and the occasional critical hit (1 in 16).
- After 20 rounds, the side with more of its total HP left wins. Equal is a draw.

### Results

| | Win | Draw | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sparks | 10 (12 in a duel) | 5 | 3 |
| XP for each card that fought | 20 | 12 | 8 |
| Rating (duels only) | up | Elo | down |
| Wild | 60% chance to keep one of the creatures you beat (80% on Wild Bloom) | – | they wander off |
| Duel | 20% chance of a bounty: a fresh card of the opponent's lead species | – | – |

- **You never lose a card in battle.** A card that faints is tired for 15 minutes. At the start of a battle, each tired slot is filled by your strongest rested card ("Tuftbun stepped in for Pipkin").
- **Catching.** When more than one wild creature was beaten, you pick one with `[1]`, `[2]` or `[3]` (the rarest is picked for you after 20 seconds). The card wobbles, then either "Gotcha!" or "It slipped away!". The server already decided; the wobble is only suspense.
- **Every battle pays.** Every finished battle pays its sparks and XP, and every wild win rolls a catch, however many you play.
- **First win of the day** adds a pack.
- **Defense.** Your saved team also defends while you are away, and defending never makes it tired. When someone's duel against it ends in their loss, you get 4 sparks and a notice.
- **Rating** starts at 1000 and moves like Elo, only in duels. The defender moves by half as much as the attacker.
- **Pairs.** Only the first 3 finished duels between the same two players in any 24 hours move rating or pay defense sparks.
- **Abandoned battles** are settled automatically after 10 minutes, as if no button was pressed.

### Opponents

- **Players.** A duel picks a random player near your rating who has a saved team and was around in the last 14 days, and never one of your last 5 opponents. They do not need to be online; you fight a snapshot of their team.
- **Rivals.** When no real player fits, a generated Rival takes the duel, always labelled `Rival` with a generated name, such as `Rival Thistlewick`. Rivals are sized to your rating and team, and duels against them move rating normally.
- **Revenge.** A notice that someone beat your team has a `[r] Revenge` button. It duels that player's current team directly, once per loss and within 24 hours, with the usual 2 minutes between duels. A revenge win pays 5 extra sparks.

## Families

There are four families, one per Claude model. A pack belongs to the family of the model you used most while it charged. The **arena** is the family of the model you are using when a battle starts, and creatures of that family fight 10% harder there, on both sides.

| Family | Colours | Style | Special |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku | greens, teals | fast, fragile | **Flurry**: two hits at 0.9x each |
| Sonnet | blues | balanced | **Couplet**: a 1.5x hit, then heal 15% of max HP |
| Opus | reds, oranges | heavy, slow | **Crescendo**: a 2.1x hit |
| Fable | purples | tricky | **Twist**: a 1.6x hit that always counts as super effective |

Opus beats Sonnet, Sonnet beats Haiku, Haiku beats Fable, and Fable beats Opus. A winning matchup hits for 1.5x, a losing one for 0.67x.

## Cards

### Every card is one of a kind

The server rolls each card's DNA when it is made. From that come:

- **Palette:** the species colour, shifted a little.
- **Pattern:** usually the species' own; sometimes spots, stripes, a belly patch, a mask or none.
- **Eyes and mouth:** dot, tall, sparkle, sleepy or fierce eyes; a smile, a fang, an "o" or no mouth.
- **Shape:** corners round off or square up, the tail may switch sides, ears and tail grow or shrink a size, and 1 in 5 cards is a runt or a chonk, so no two silhouettes are quite the same.
- **Trinket:** a 5% chance of a tiny hat, bow, flower, scarf or monocle.
- **Genes:** four values from 0 to 15 that nudge hp, attack, defense and speed by up to 12% either way. The **gene score** is their total as a percentage.
- **Traits:** one, or two for epic and legendary cards.

**Shiny** cards (1 in 100) take the other half of their family's colours, with gold accents and a twinkle, and their frame sparkles. **Foil** is a separate holographic finish, rolled at 1 in 16 on any card: a rainbow band sweeps across the creature and the border cycles through the rainbow. Every legendary and every Mythic is foil. Foil never changes stats. A card can be both: `Rare · Shiny Foil`.

### Traits

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sturdy | Survives the first lethal hit with 1 HP, once per battle |
| Swift | +15% speed |
| Thick Hide | Takes 10% less damage |
| Lucky Star | Critical hits at 1 in 8 instead of 1 in 16 |
| Quick Charge | Special ready after 1 normal attack instead of 2 |
| Glass Heart | +20% attack, −15% hp |
| Regrowth | Heals 6% of max HP at the end of each round it is active |
| Underdog | +25% attack while below 30% HP |
| Ambush | +30% damage on its first action after entering |
| Moonlit | Specials heal it for 25% of the damage they deal |
| Stubborn | Ignores type disadvantage |
| Showoff | Specials deal 25% more |
| Guardian | When it faints, the next ally gets +15% defense for the rest of the battle |
| Sleepy | −10% speed, +15% hp |
| Homebody | Its arena bonus is 1.2x instead of 1.1x |
| Mimic | Its special copies the special of the opposing creature's family |

### Levels, evolution and raised forms

- Cards level from 1 to 10; the next level needs 40 × the current level in XP.
- **Three stages.** A card evolves on its own at level 4 and again at level 8, and takes a new name each time, such as Sootwolf, then Sootaptor, then Sootinotaur. Stage 2 is 15% stronger than stage 1 and wears its species' accessory (horns, a crown, antennae, a halo, spikes or wings); stage 3 is 30% stronger, with a grander accessory and one new part it lacked, such as a tail or wings.
- **Final forms.** Legendaries and Mythics never evolve: they arrive in their final form.
- **Raised forms.** How a card evolves depends on how it was raised. At its first evolution, its stage-2 form takes the accessory style and a light tint of the arena family it battled in most, and the second evolution deepens the same look, so one species can grow up four different ways, such as an "Opus-raised Pipmaw". Ties, and a card that never battled, go to the card's own family. Your card shows it as `Raised under Opus`.
- **Starters** begin at level 3, 20 XP short of level 4, so one good battle evolves them.
- **Power** (shown on cards) is hp + 2 × attack + 2 × defense + speed.

### First discoveries

A species you see for the first time gets a `NEW` badge, and its album cell fills in. In the online world, the first card of a species that anyone in the world obtains in a season gets a permanent `First Discovered` stamp. When a wild encounter could be a world first, the reveal teases `★ FIRST IN THE WORLD?`.

### Mythics

About 1 wild encounter in 40 is led by a **Mythic**: a creature generated on the spot from a fresh seed, with its own body, a palette from a random family, a two-word name such as "Hollowmere Duskwing", and stats above a legendary's. It has never existed before and never will again.

- The normal catch roll applies. If you lose or abandon the battle, it vanishes forever.
- Online, a caught Mythic carries `Mythic · 1 of 1` and `Discovered by` your handle, and it appears on the public "Mythics found" list (handle and name only). Offline, it is a `Local mythic` and makes no such claim.
- It counts as legendary for rarity and trading, and it is always foil.

## Packs

A pack holds 5 cards.

| Slots | Common | Rare | Epic | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | 70% | 22% | 7% | 1% |
| 5 | – | 75% | 21% | 4% |

A legendary roll gives the family's legendary species; any other roll picks one of the family's 8 regular species. Each card also rolls shiny (1 in 100) and foil (1 in 16) on its own.

**Charging.** Every 50 minutes that Claude Code is open on your machine, idle time included, a pack charges in the family you used most in that stretch. Charges are at least 45 minutes apart (90 minutes beyond 16 charges in any 24 hours, which no person reaches). You can hold 12 unopened packs; when the bank is full, the game says "Open some packs to make room".

**Other packs:** your first win of the day, every 3rd win in a row, season rewards, the Wandering Trader, drops, a gift claimed by a new player once they have really played, and buying them for 150 sparks each, as many as you like.

**Opening.** The cards are rolled when you open the pack, not when it charges. Five cards appear face down, and each back glows in its rarity colour before it flips: blue for rare, purple for epic, pulsing gold for legendary. `[f]` flips the next one, and they flip on their own anyway, taking longer over the rarer ones.

## Fusion

Fuse any two of your cards into one brand-new hybrid. Both parents are used up.

- **Body:** the first parent's torso, legs and tail, with the second parent's head top, wings and eye spacing. **Family** is the second parent's, and the hybrid wears both parents' colours.
- **Name:** a blend of both parents' names, with one for each stage. The same parents always give the same names.
- **Rarity** is the higher parent's, with a 15% chance of one tier up (never into legendary).
- **Traits:** one from each parent where possible. **Genes:** the parents' average, plus a little noise.
- **Level:** one below the parents' average (at least 1), at the stage that level reaches. Hybrids evolve normally.
- **Foil** if both parents are foil; otherwise it rolls 1 in 16.
- Each hybrid gets fresh DNA, so two fusions of the same parents never look the same.
- Costs 40 sparks (20 on Fusion Fair days); the cost is the only limit. Bound cards and cards held for a trade or gift cannot be fused. Every hybrid you make goes in your **Fusion Log**.

## Crafting and recycling

- **Craft** any current-season species from the Album with fresh DNA: 50, 200, 800 or 3200 sparks for common, rare, epic or legendary.
- **Recycle** a card for 4, 15, 60 or 250 sparks by rarity. Shiny doubles it, foil pays 1.5x, and a Mythic doubles once more. Bound cards cannot be recycled. A pack's worth of cards recycles for well under the 150 sparks a pack costs.

## Trading, the market and gifts

Trading, the market and gifts are online only. Any online account can use them from its first day: there is no waiting period, no fee and no lock on cards that change hands. Only bound cards (starters and cards from bound drops) never trade.

### Offers

- Open a player's profile (the opponent you just battled, someone on the trade board, or `/spin trade <handle>`) to see their cards marked **for trade**.
- Offer 1 to 3 of your cards for 0 to 3 of theirs. Your cards are held for the trade until the other player accepts, declines or counters, or the offer expires after 72 hours.
- On accept, the swap is atomic and costs nothing. Traded cards leave teams, and received cards can be traded on at once.
- You can have up to 50 offers waiting at once. Bound cards, and cards held for another trade, a gift or the market, cannot be offered.

### The market

- Open Community → Market, or use `/spin market`. Your listings has its own filter within the market.
- **Sell a card** for sparks, for a card you want, or both. The card waits on the market, off your team, until someone buys it, you take it back, or 14 days pass. Your last team card cannot go on the market.
- **What you can ask for:** a price in sparks, and/or a card: a species, or a family and a lowest rarity, and if you like only shiny or only foil.
- **Buying** works any time, even while the seller is away. You pay the price and, if the listing wants a card, hand over one of yours that fits. Every spark goes to the seller; there is no fee. If two players buy at once, one gets it and the other is told it is already sold.
- **Browse** by family, rarity, species, shiny, foil, kind and price, newest first, cheapest or priciest. Each species shows its last 5 sale prices, with the day and no names.
- You can have up to 100 cards on the market at once. A listing shows your handle, the card, the price, what you want and the day you listed it.

### The trade board

Open Community → Trading → Trade board. Mark cards **for trade** and keep a **wishlist** of up to 5 species. The board shows up to 20 matches: players who have a for-trade card you want and want a species you have marked. With no matches, it shows recent for-trade cards instead, and the Wandering Trader's deals are always there.

### The Wandering Trader

A non-player trader with 3 deals each UTC day, picked by the date. For example: 2 cards of one family for 1 rare of another; 1 epic for 2 rares of the deal's family; 5 cards of any kind for 1 pack. Each deal is in stock once per player per UTC day, it uses up the cards you give, and the server checks every deal. The Trader deals offline too.

### Gifts

- `/spin gift <card>` holds the card and gives you a code like `quiet-otter-lamp-4821`, valid for 14 days, plus a link to a page showing the card and how to claim it.
- Anyone with Spinlings can claim it with `/spin claim <code>`, however new their account. The card arrives as a wrapped present, free to trade on at once. You cannot claim your own gift.
- Unclaimed gifts come back to you after 14 days.
- If the person who claims it joined after you made the gift, you get a bonus pack once they have finished 5 battles on 2 different days.
- You can have 10 gifts waiting at once, and a player can try 5 claim codes an hour.

| Spend | |
|---|---|

New players start with 100 sparks. Sparks cannot be bought or cashed out for money; on the market they only pass between players.

## Streaks, leagues and seasons

- **Streaks.** Wins in a row, wild or duel, build a streak; a loss or draw resets it. Every 3rd consecutive win pays a streak pack. During a battle the band shows `streak 2 · one more!`.
- **Leagues** come from your rating: Pebble from 0, Brook from 1100, Grove from 1300, Peak from 1500 and Star from 1700. Crossing a league line, either way, gets a short badge moment.
- **Seasons** last 28 days; season 1 started on 2026-10-01. Each brings 36 new species: 8 regular and 1 legendary per family. Cards from past seasons stay yours and keep battling and trading; crafting covers the current season only.
- **Season end.** On your first visit of a new season, you get reward packs by your final league (1 for Pebble, 2 for Brook, 3 for Grove, 4 for Peak and 5 for Star, which also brings a guaranteed foil legendary), and your rating moves halfway back toward 1000.
- **Leaderboards.** Community → Rankings, or `/spin leaderboard`, shows the top players, all time or this season: by rating, players beaten, duel wins, species collected, Mythics found and market sales, with your own rank. Every player is on them unless they leave with `/spin leaderboard off`, which also takes their stats off their profile. Rivals never appear.
- **Challenges.** You can duel a particular player's team by their handle, with the usual 2 minutes between duels. Only the first 3 duels between the same two players in any 24 hours move rating or count toward duel stats.

## The living world

Every UTC day has one rule for every battle, the same for everyone:

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| Haiku Day | Haiku creatures are 20% faster |
| Sonnet Day | Sonnet creatures heal 4% each round |
| Opus Day | Opus creatures hit 15% harder |
| Fable Day | Fable specials are ready after one charge |
| Topsy-Turvy | The family cycle runs backwards |
| Glass Day | Critical hits deal double |
| Long Day | Battles run to 30 rounds |
| Gentle Day | All damage is 15% lower |
| Wild Bloom | Catches succeed 80% of the time |
| Shiny Hour | From 18:00 to 19:00 UTC, shinies are 1 in 25 |
| Fusion Fair | Fusion costs 20 sparks |
| Calm Day | No rule |

- **Featured species.** Each day one of the season's species is featured and turns up in 15% of wild slots.
- **Weekly roamer.** Each week one of the season's legendaries roams, leading 1% of wild encounters. It is the rarest encounter in the game, and the band border turns gold when it is near: "The air feels different…".
- The first time you open the pane each day, a banner shows the rule and the featured species.

## Drops

Now and then a code such as `FOUNDERS` is posted. `/spin redeem <code>` hatches the drop's creature with your own DNA, so everyone's copy looks different, or opens its packs. Each account redeems a drop once. Most drop cards are bound, so they stay with you.

## Your account and the two worlds

- **Online** is the default, on [spinlings.dev](https://spinlings.dev). The mod joins on its own the first time you open Claude Code and gives you a random handle such as `soft-otter-42`. `/spin handle` shows your handle, and `/spin handle new` draws a new one once a week. Generated handles remain the default.
- **Your public username.** Save a passkey, then use Community → Profile → Open your collection to sign in to the browser page. You can choose a unique name of 1–40 ASCII letters, numbers, `_` or `-`; it is saved in lowercase and passes the game's name filter, with names such as `admin` and `support` reserved. Choosing a name and drawing a generated handle share the once-a-week limit. Old names are held for 30 days. Your account, cards and passkeys stay the same. The browser otherwise shows your game; open packs, change your team and trade in Claude Code. Your chosen name is public, so reusing a name from elsewhere can identify you.
- **Offline** sends nothing, ever: wild encounters, catches, packs, evolution, fusion, crafting, Mythics (as a `Local mythic`), Rivals and the Trader all work from a save on your machine. Duels with real players, trading, gifts, drops, the leaderboard and First Discovered need the online world.
- **Picking a world.** You start online. If the first run cannot reach the server, the game starts you offline and says so. After that, `/spin world online` or `/spin world offline` switches at any time. Each world keeps its own collection; cards never move between them, and switching deletes nothing.
- **Another computer.** `/spin devices` saves a passkey, and another computer signs in with it. Without a passkey, losing every device loses the online account.
- **Community servers.** `/spin server <url>` plays on a server someone else runs, after a one-time notice that it is not spinlings.dev. Each server is a separate world with its own account and collection, and nothing moves between servers; `/spin server default` comes back. [self-hosting.md](self-hosting.md) shows how to run one.
- `/spin privacy` shows what the mod has sent, resets access on every device, and deletes your account. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
